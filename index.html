What We Offer

Rehabilitation built on performance.

Physical Therapy

1-on-1 sessions with a Doctor of Physical Therapy tailored to you and the specific demands of your sport, recreation, or regular activity.

Athletic Screening

Whether it's preseason, postseason, or in season, a dual-credentialed Doctor of Physical Therapy and Strength and Conditioning Specialist can help you identify key performance indicators and achieve your best results.

Return-to-Sport

Comprehensive assessments after injury using advanced testing gear, including force plate metrics, designed to get you back to full activity safely and confidently.

Recovery

Modeled after high level training rooms, Matador has the tools to help you recover faster. Available with all Physical Therapy appointments or as a cash-based subscription.

Dry Needling

Manage pain better with dry needling to get you back to activity faster than ever. Available with all Physical Therapy appointments or as a standalone cash-based service.

Concussion Rehabilitation

Finding the right care for a concussion can be intimidating. At Matador, our Physical Therapist has the additional training and equipment to guide you through the recovery process to safely return to full activity.

Whether you took a hard hit in a football game, experienced a motor vehicle accident, or had an injury years ago you still feel is affecting you, being assessed for a concussion can give you the direction and peace of mind you need.

About Us

Colby is a Doctor of Physical Therapy, Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist, and Doctoral Candidate. He graduated with his Bachelor’s from Utah Valley University in 2019 and his Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) from Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center in 2023. He continues to attend Texas Tech pursuing his Doctoral of Science (Sc.D) in Rehabilitation Sciences with a research emphasis on return to sport assessments.

Colby has extensive training in multiple aspects of athlete management, including targeted rehabilitation, strength training, sport-specific key performance indicator assessments, and post-concussion evaluation and treatment. Based heavily in athlete performance, he approaches rehabilitation and training with a blend of sport science, objective performance metrics, and comprehensive return to sport programming.

In his free time, Colby enjoys spending time with his wife Liz, his son Neil, and his dog Rex. He enjoys spending time outdoors, hiking, and going on road trips. He is also an avid fan of multiple sports and loves helping athletes reach their potential and compete at their best.
