What We Offer

Rehabilitation built on performance.

Physical Therapy

1-on-1 sessions with a Doctor of Physical Therapy tailored to you and the specific demands of your sport, recreation, or regular activity.

Athletic Screening

Whether it's preseason, postseason, or in season, a dual-credentialed Doctor of Physical Therapy and Strength and Conditioning Specialist can help you identify key performance indicators and achieve your best results.

Return-to-Sport

Comprehensive assessments after injury using advanced testing gear, including force plate metrics, designed to get you back to full activity safely and confidently.

Recovery

Modeled after high level training rooms, Matador has the tools to help you recover faster. Available with all Physical Therapy appointments or as a cash-based subscription.

Dry Needling

TManage pain better with dry needling to get you back to activity faster than ever. Available with all Physical Therapy appointments or as a standalone cash-based service.
